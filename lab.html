Query triage

Automation
The situation
Questions arrive in one general queue. Some are complicated and need a person. Many are people asking where to find something or who to speak to, and a person answers those on autopilot.
A better way
A front door reads each query first. Simple ones get pointed to the right page or person, anything urgent or sensitive goes straight to a human, and every query is tagged, so after a month you can see what people are really asking.
What I built
Safety Desk, a demo that answers health and safety questions only from public UK law and HSE guidance, with a source on every answer and a handover to a person for anything urgent or sensitive. Next is an insights view that shows the tagged queries, on sample data.
