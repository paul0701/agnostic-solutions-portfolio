Pawn Force

Chess endless runner

A chess endless runner. You are a pawn on a three lane board, and the pieces coming at you move like the real thing: rooks charge, bishops slide along the diagonals, knights hop in an L. Your score is your chess rating.

Play Pawn Force

Blockchain Breakout

Endless runner

You are a coin escaping a blockchain. Run, dodge and collect gems while set pieces hit mid run: a scanner drone and a collapsing floor (the Rug Pull), a wrong chain that halves your score (the Halving) and screen interference with phantom obstacles (the Hack).

Play Blockchain Breakout Has a leaderboard

Guess the Opening

Daily chess game

Moves appear one at a time and you hit “I know it” the moment you recognise the opening. Pick it from four answers, then the variation. A wrong answer wipes your multiplier. There is a new opening every day, like Wordle, and a free play mode for practice.

Play Guess the Opening Has a leaderboard

Slash & Dash

Arcade runner

A side scrolling arcade runner with a Halloween theme and a Christmas theme you can switch between mid run. Dodge the cops, grab power-ups and build up to the blade boost. Install it to your home screen and it plays offline.

Play Slash & Dash Has a leaderboard

Vertical Limit

Tap to climb

Tap the next grip and climb while rocks fall from above. At every base camp you can bank your points and stop, or push on and risk losing the lot.

Play Vertical Limit Leaderboard coming

Courier Run

Arcade runner

Be the paperboy. Jump the dogs tearing across the road and land your papers on the doorsteps at the same time. Miss five deliveries and the round is over, so the trick is doing both at once.
